Critical Risks & Mitigations

🎥 D-ID Integration HIGH

Issue: No native iOS SDK available
Solution: WKWebView wrapper with JavaScript bridge
Fallback: Video-only mode (no real-time streaming)

💳 Payment Migration MEDIUM

Issue: Apple requires StoreKit for digital purchases
Impact: 30% revenue share to Apple
Solution: Backend sync between Stripe & StoreKit

🏪 App Store Review MEDIUM

Issue: Religious/spiritual content scrutiny
Solution: 17+ rating, clear disclaimers
Prep: "Not professional advice" notices

🔊 Background Audio LOW

Issue: Apple scrutinizes background audio usage
Solution: Proper AVAudioSession configuration
Prep: Only enable during active meditation
